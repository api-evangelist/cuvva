# Cuvva

> Cuvva is a UK insurtech offering flexible, short-term car and van insurance through its mobile apps — from one-hour to 28-day policies, learner-driver cover, drive-away cover, and a subscription (Flexi) product. Cuvva exposes a fleet of small single-purpose service APIs (auth, vehicle, MOT, motor-coverage, billing, promo, profile, and more) documented publicly on GitHub, secured with its own OAuth 2.0 implementation and JWT bearer tokens.

## APIs

- [Cuvva services API docs](https://github.com/cuvva/docs/tree/master/apis): Public documentation for Cuvva's service APIs (auth, vehicle, mot, motor-coverage, billing, promo, profile, upload, terms, notification, and more).
- Production base URL: https://api.prod.cuv-prod.app
- Sandbox base URL: https://api.sandbox.cuv-nonprod.app
- Auth: OAuth 2.0 (service-auth) with JWT bearer access tokens and rotating refresh tokens.
